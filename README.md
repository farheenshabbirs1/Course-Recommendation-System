## Overview

The **Course Mapping System** is a web-based application developed for the University of the Pacific's Office of Global Education to simplify the process of finding and reviewing international course equivalencies.

The application centralizes course mapping data and provides students and advisors with a searchable interface to identify pre-approved international courses, review equivalencies, export results, and submit course approval requests.

The system was designed with a focus on **usability, maintainability, data integration, and application performance**.

---

## Problem

Before the system was introduced, students had to:

- Search individual partner university websites
- Manually compare international course catalogs
- Determine potential UOP course equivalencies
- Submit course approval information manually
- Repeatedly contact advisors for course-mapping information

This created unnecessary manual work and made it difficult for students to quickly identify previously approved course equivalencies.
